# Competitors

This covers the five products on the Liinks compare page, plus Liinks itself. It was researched on 2026-10-31 from public pages, without using the products while logged in. The full list of features is in [features.md](features.md).

## What each one is

| Product | What it's really for | Price | Fees on sales | What to learn from it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liinks | Well-designed pages with many customisation options, and agencies managing profiles | $5 Starter, $12 Pro per 5 profiles. No free plan | No store | Closest to Tondo. Strong on block range, QR codes, a public API and an MCP server |
| Linktree | The default choice, with a large marketplace of link apps | Free, then about $8, $15, $35 (reviews, not confirmed on its pricing page) | Reviews say 12% on Free, 9% on Starter and Pro, 0% on Premium | App marketplace, 40+ social icons, mobile apps, priority links |
| Beacons | An all-in-one creator business: store, email, media kit, AI | Free, $10, $30, $100 | 9% on Free and Creator, 0% above | Media kit, invoices, brand deals, AI tools, Instagram auto-DMs, NFC card |
| Stan | Selling courses, coaching and downloads | $29, $99. No free plan | 0%, plus payment processing | Product types, checkout, upsells, email flows, payment plans |
| Later | A social scheduling tool, where link in bio is a side feature | Free link page. Liinks says scheduling plans run $25 to $110 | Not stated | Links tied to individual posts, up to five per Instagram post |
| Shorby (now Shor) | Marketers using short links and retargeting | $15, $29, $99 per a June 2026 comparison. Other sources conflict | No checkout | Messenger buttons, pixels for seven ad platforms, feeds from Shopify, Etsy, WordPress and RSS |

## Where each stands against Tondo's goals

- **Design:** Liinks is the real design competitor. The others treat design as secondary, and that's still Tondo's opening.
- **Selling:** Stan and Beacons are far ahead, and each is a large product. Competing with them is a long-term goal.
- **Reach:** Linktree wins on awareness and integrations. Importing from it matters, because most people switching will come from there.
- **Teams:** Liinks and Shorby have multi-profile management, and Linktree keeps it for Enterprise, according to Liinks. No product here appears to offer shared looks yet. Liinks lists "share themes between profiles" as coming soon, so this advantage may not last.
- **Art:** none of them generate a page's artwork. That's unchanged.

## Where the Liinks compare page can't be trusted

The page is written by Liinks, and some of it conflicts with the competitors' own pages or other sources:

| Liinks says | Other evidence |
|---|---|
| Linktree has no Instagram post sync | Linktree's marketplace lists an Instagram app that displays posts and reels |
| Beacons has no scheduled links | Beacons' pricing page lists scheduling and automation |
| Linktree has no custom domain | Reviews say Pro and Premium have one |
| Stan has no custom date range, form or embed blocks | Not confirmed either way |

So features.md counts a feature as present when any source shows it. It doesn't take a "no" from Liinks as settled.

## Limits of this research

- **Linktree:** its marketplace page shows only 20 of about 64 listings, so that list is incomplete. Its pricing page shows no prices.
- **Stan:** its home and pricing pages returned nothing usable, so plan details come from Stan's own pricing article.
- **Shorby:** its pricing page was empty, so details come from a third-party comparison. Sources disagree on price.
- **Later:** pricing and scheduling details come partly from Liinks.
- **Beacons:** the page was cut off partway and repeats sections with different values. Treat its per-plan limits with caution.
- **No hands-on use:** behaviour inside these editors hasn't been checked, so a real audit would need paid trials. Features that exist but aren't advertised will be missing.
