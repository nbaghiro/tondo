Tondo design languages

Five marketing page studies with the same copy, so the comparison is about design. Prices, features and people are placeholders, and every painting is made from CSS and SVG.

SalonPainted gallery walls with gilded round frames. Formal, rich and memorable. PigmentFlat colour fields and huge overlapping circles. Loud, graphic and confident. WashWatercolour blooms on pale paper. Soft and friendly, and closest to Liinks. CutoutHand-cut paper shapes in the spirit of Matisse. Playful and warm. EclipseA glowing ring against deep violet night. Dramatic and modern.

Wash variations

Five takes on Wash, with paintings that have darker edges and paper grain. Each has its own palette, type, borders and layout.

IndigoBlue on cool white. Thin ink outlines, square corners and a printed offset shadow. MadderRose and sienna on blush. Soft serif type and wobbly, hand-drawn borders. SapGreens and ochre on pale celadon. Arched cards like garden windows, with bold outlines. DuskGlowing paint on midnight blue. Tall condensed serif, with glass cards and fine light borders. SumiBlack ink rings on white with one red accent. No boxes, only underlines.
